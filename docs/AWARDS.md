## Competition Phases

### Phase 1: Exam & Video
- Technical exam on exact sciences (mathematics, physics, etc.)
- Video presentation explaining the chosen SDG (Sustainable Development Goal) and project concept
- Passing this phase qualified us for the national finals

### Phase 2: Prototyping
- UI/UX design in Figma
- Detailed project documentation
- Technical specifications

### Phase 3: Development Week
- Intensive week at ITA in São José dos Campos
- App development using MIT App Inventor
- Physical prototype creation with Arduino
- International Project Exhibition
- Final presentation to judges panel
- Award ceremony

---

## Impact

**SDG Focus**: SDG 13 (Climate Action) and SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities)

**Social Impact**:
- Addresses a critical problem affecting thousands of Brazilian families annually
- Provides early warning system for vulnerable communities
- Combines technology with social responsibility
- Demonstrates youth engagement in solving real-world problems
